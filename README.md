# stocks

Quant research and paper-trading playground for US stocks and ETFs.

**Current focus:** a "follow the options money" scanner. Every day it ranks sectors and stocks by where price action and options pricing *disagree*:
- **Bullish divergence:** price weak, but options getting calmer and less hedged.
- **Bearish divergence:** price strong, but traders paying up for downside protection.

The next step is backtesting whether that divergence actually predicts returns.

See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for layout, setup and rules.

> Research and education only. Not investment advice. Backtested and paper results are hypothetical.
